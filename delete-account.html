Delete Your QuitSure Account

You can permanently delete your QuitSure account and all associated cloud data.

Delete From the App

  1. Open QuitSure.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Select Delete Account.
  4. Confirm the deletion.

Unable to Access the App?

Send an email to:

support@quitsure.app

Include:

  • Your registered email address
  • Subject: Delete My QuitSure Account

Data That Will Be Deleted

  • User account
  • Cloud backup
  • Habit history
  • Daily logs
  • Statistics
  • User preferences stored online

Data That May Be Retained

Anonymous analytics, crash reports, and information required by law may be retained for a limited period.

Deletion Time

Requests are normally completed within 30 days.

Questions?

Contact:
